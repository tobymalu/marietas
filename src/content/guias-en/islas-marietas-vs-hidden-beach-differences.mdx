> **Quick answer:** the **Islas Marietas** are the whole archipelago, a National Park with reefs, seabirds and beaches. **Hidden Beach** is just one beach inside a crater on one of the islands, with restricted access. Every tour visits the Islas Marietas, but **only some go into Hidden Beach**.

## Are the Islas Marietas and Hidden Beach the same thing?

It's the most common mix-up before booking. The Islas Marietas are a volcanic archipelago off Punta de Mita, protected as a National Park and Biosphere Reserve. Its two main islands are **Isla Redonda** and **Isla Larga**, surrounded by islets, rock arches and coral reefs.

**Hidden Beach** is inside Isla Redonda, in a crater open to the sky. To get there you swim through a rock tunnel, and CONANP limits how many people can enter each day. That's why a tour "to the Islas Marietas" doesn't always include Hidden Beach: it's a separate permit, with its own time slot and rules.

## Who is the classic tour best for?

The classic snorkel tour explores the archipelago from the outside and stops at **La Nopalera Beach**, a white-sand beach with crystal-clear water on Isla Larga. It's ideal if:

- **You're traveling as a family or with kids:** there's no age limit, plus child and infant rates.
- **You want a relaxed day:** snorkeling, kayaking and paddleboarding at your own pace, with breakfast, lunch and an open bar on the way back.
- **You're watching your budget:** it costs less than the Hidden Beach tour and you get almost the same day.
- **You're not a confident swimmer:** you can enjoy the ocean with a life jacket without crossing the tunnel.

## Who is the Hidden Beach tour best for?

The Hidden Beach tour is **the same full day as the classic tour, plus 30 to 45 minutes inside the crater**. It's worth it if:

- **You're traveling as a couple or with friends** and want the most iconic photo of the Islas Marietas.
- **You're adventurous and a good swimmer:** swimming in through the tunnel is part of the experience.
- **You love photography:** the light pouring in through the open crater is unique.
- **You can book ahead:** spots are assigned by CONANP and are the first to sell out.
- **Everyone in your group is between 12 and 60**, which is the entry requirement.

If you've already chosen the beach, read our guide on [how to visit Hidden Beach](/en/guide/activities/how-to-visit-hidden-beach-islas-marietas/), with requirements, opening days and tips.

## How to choose in 30 seconds

1. **Is anyone in your group under 12 or over 60?** → Classic tour.
2. **Is anyone not a strong swimmer?** → Classic tour.
3. **Want to see Hidden Beach and traveling Wednesday to Sunday?** → Hidden Beach tour.
4. **Is your date a Tuesday?** → Classic tour: Hidden Beach is closed on Mondays and Tuesdays. On Mondays the whole park is closed and there are no departures.
