> **Quick answer:** you can only reach Hidden Beach on a **tour with an authorized operator** that has spots assigned by CONANP. You get in by **swimming through a tunnel** wearing a life jacket, daily spots are very limited and access normally runs **Wednesday to Sunday**. Book ahead and check which fees are paid at the pier.

## Why is access to Hidden Beach limited?

The Islas Marietas have been a **National Park since 2005** and a **UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2008**. In 2016 CONANP temporarily closed Hidden Beach because too many visitors were damaging the reef and the sand. Since it reopened, it works with a **daily carrying capacity**: only a small number of people can enter each day, in small groups and at assigned times.

That is why there are no "walk-in tickets" for the beach: spots are assigned to authorized operators, who share them among their guests. If someone offers to get you in on your own, be careful.

## Requirements to enter Hidden Beach

- **You must know how to swim.** The last stretch is a swim through a natural tunnel; at high tide or with big swells, access may be suspended for safety.
- **A life jacket is mandatory**, and you must follow your guide's instructions at all times.
- **Be between 12 and 60 years old.** Children under 12 and adults over 60 are not allowed in.
- **Responsible sun protection:** only biodegradable sunscreen is allowed. Even better, wear a UV rash guard.
- **Take nothing:** no sand, shells, coral or wildlife.

## Where do tours depart from?

The Islas Marietas lie off Punta de Mita, in Banderas Bay. Tours leave from **Puerto Vallarta**, **Nuevo Vallarta**, **La Cruz de Huanacaxtle** and **Punta de Mita / Sayulita**: the further north you start, the shorter the boat ride. Our tours depart at 9:00 AM from the Puerto Vallarta Cruise Terminal, with an alternate pickup at the Nuevo Vallarta pier; details are on each tour page.

## Local tips before you book

1. **Book ahead**, especially during holidays and weekends: Hidden Beach spots sell out first.
2. **Ask what the price includes** and what is paid at the pier. A very low price usually leaves out the bracelet or the taxes.
3. **Check the day of the week:** Hidden Beach is normally closed on Mondays and Tuesdays.
4. **Have a plan B:** if the sea is rough, CONANP can close access on the same day. The classic tour is still a great experience.
5. **Bring the essentials:** swimsuit on, rash guard, towel, water and a dry bag for your phone.
