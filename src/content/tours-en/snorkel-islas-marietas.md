A full day to swim, paddle and discover the marine life of the Islas Marietas, without entering Hidden Beach.

## Itinerary

1. Check-in at the pier and payment of the port tax and the protected area bracelet.
2. Breakfast on board while the other guests board.
3. Boat ride to the Islas Marietas National Park.
4. Group activities: snorkeling, kayaking and paddleboarding.
5. Visit to Nopalera Beach with free time to swim.
6. Full lunch on board and an open bar on the way back, once outside the protected area.

## What's included

- Round-trip boat ride from Puerto Vallarta.
- Snorkeling gear (mask, fins and life jacket).
- Kayaks and paddleboards.
- Certified guide.
- Breakfast and lunch on board.
- Open bar on the way back.

## Not included

- National park conservation fee (approx. $180 MXN).
- Port tax (approx. $30 MXN).
- Photos and souvenirs.
- Tips for the crew.

## Schedule

- **Departure:** 8:00 AM from Puerto Vallarta.
- **Duration:** about 8 hours.
- **Days:** Wednesday to Sunday, subject to sea conditions.

Weather or tide conditions may limit access to some areas of the islands. Bookings must be made at least 24 hours in advance.
